1. Pre-Execution Price Impact & Sandwich Guard

Solana memecoin bonding curves operate on constant product AMM mechanics ($k = x \cdot y$). Large trade sizes in shallow curves cause severe price impact, allowing MEV searchers to execute sandwich attacks. Sandwich Guard calculates the impact before submission:

$$\text{Price Impact (\%)} = \left(\frac{\Delta SOL}{vSOL_{\text{curve}} + \Delta SOL}\right) \times 100$$

• If Price Impact $\ge 4.0\%$: Slippage is automatically clamped from 15% to 5%, and trades are split into micro-orders.

3. Sybil Multi-Wallet Cluster Detector

  • High-velocity bursts: $\ge 5$ distinct wallets purchasing in under 2 seconds.

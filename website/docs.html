2. Telegram Phone Remote Controller

Control your trading bot from your phone with long-polling commands:

/status - Bankroll, win rate & realized PnL
/positions - Open positions & unrealized PnL
/vault - Locked profit summary & completed cycles
/strategy <NAME> - Hot-swap active strategy
/panic - 🚨 Emergency sell all open positions into SOL
/sell <MINT> - Liquidate a single token position

Frequently Asked Questions

        
Everything you need to know about the Quantum trading agent.

      


      

        

          
What is Paper Trading Mode?

          

            Paper trading connects directly to live Solana WebSocket price feeds and simulates trade entries, dynamic risk management, and profit milestones with virtual SOL, ensuring zero financial risk while testing strategies.
          

        


        

          
How does Jito MEV protection work?

          

            Transactions are submitted directly to Jito Block Engines across 5 global regions as private bundles with a dynamic tip instruction, completely bypassing the public mempool to make sandwich attacks impossible.
          

        


        

          
Which AI models are supported?

          

            The AI Brain supports GitHub Copilot (via local proxy at http://localhost:4141), Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Google Gemini 1.5, DeepSeek R1 (via local Ollama), OpenAI GPT-4o, and an offline built-in neural heuristics engine.
